Frequently asked questions

I’ve never done this before. Is that okay?

Yes! Workshops are designed for complete beginners. I guide every step.

What’s the minimum group size for a private party?

Private parties start at a small group. Share your headcount in the booking form and I’ll suggest the best format.

Can you come to our location?

Yes. I bring everything needed to your home, office or venue in the Los Angeles area, and can travel further for larger groups.
