Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I reserve my date?

After we’ve talked, a signed agreement and a retainer reserve your date. Dates aren’t held until the retainer is received.

What areas do you serve?

Los Angeles and Southern California, with travel available anywhere. Travel costs are included in your quote.

Can I book more than one service?

Yes! Many couples pair live wedding painting with custom stationery art, or add a bridal-party workshop. Select everything you’re interested in on the form.

What is your cancellation policy?

Retainers are non-refundable because I turn away other bookings for your date, but they can often be moved to a new date. Full details are in your agreement.
