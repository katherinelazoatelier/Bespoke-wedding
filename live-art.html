Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How long do you paint for?

Most events are 2–5 hours. I’ll recommend the right length for the scene and canvas you choose.

Can guests interact with you while you paint?

Absolutely. Guests love asking questions and watching up close. For brand events I can also invite guests to add a brushstroke.

Can the painting include our logo or products?

Yes. Branded live art is a great fit for launches and activations. We’ll plan it together on our pre-event call.

Do you need a deposit?

A retainer reserves your date, and the balance is due before the event.
