Frequently asked questions

How long does a commission take?

Most commissions take 3–6 weeks depending on size and medium. Rush timelines may be available, so just ask.

Can I give a commission as a gift?

Yes, commissions make wonderful wedding, anniversary and holiday gifts. Let me know your deadline when you inquire.

Do you ship?

Yes, finished pieces are carefully packed and shipped with tracking.
