Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should we book?

Most couples book 6–12 months ahead. Peak-season Saturdays go first, so inquire as soon as you have your date and venue.

Do you travel?

Yes. I’m based in Los Angeles and travel throughout California and beyond. Travel fees are included in your custom quote.

What do you need on the day?

A small area about 6×6 feet with a good view of the moment we’re painting, and access to an outlet for evening lighting if indoors.

When will we receive our painting?

Most paintings are finished, varnished and delivered within 3–5 weeks of your wedding.

How do we reserve our date?

A signed agreement and a retainer reserve your date. The balance is due before the wedding.
