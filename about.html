Meet the artist

Hi, I’m Katherine.

I’m an artist based in Los Angeles, and I believe the most meaningful moments deserve to be made by hand.

In the studio
My story

Art that brings people together

I work in watercolor, acrylic and fabric. I paint live at weddings and events, design surface patterns, and host workshops where people slow down and make something with their own hands.

What ties it all together is connection: the couple seeing their first dance on canvas, the friends laughing over embroidery hoops, the client who hangs a painting of the home where they raised their family.

Every piece I make is one of a kind, because every story is.
